Exam Priority Scoring Formula

Transparent, explainable academic trend prediction

Priority Score (0–100) = Frequency (40%) + Marks Weight (25%) + Recency (15%) + Gap Comeback Bonus (20%)
40%
1. Exam Frequency (Consistency): Measures how consistently the topic appeared across multiple exam years. Core evergreen syllabus topics carry up to 40 points.
25%
2. Total Marks Weightage: Calculates cumulative marks carried by questions in this topic relative to standard question weight (typically 5 to 15 marks).
15%
3. Recency Weight: Topics tested in the most recent exam cycle carry higher baseline relevance.
20%
4. Gap Comeback Bonus: A statistical pattern observed in multi-year examinations: frequent topics skipped in the immediately preceding 1–2 cycles have a high probability of rotational return!
🔥 High Priority (Score ≥ 65): Must-study core chapters
⚡ Medium Priority (Score 42–64): Expected standard questions
🌱 Low Priority (Score < 42): Secondary / optional concepts
Academic Note: This is a statistical trend-based recommendation based on historical question paper analysis to optimize study time. It is not an official guarantee by the examination board.
